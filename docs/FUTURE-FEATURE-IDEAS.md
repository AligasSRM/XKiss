# XKiss Future Feature Ideas

## 🟡 HIGH INTEREST — XKiss Connect

**Status:** IDEA / UNDER FUTURE REVIEW  
**Priority:** HIGH INTEREST  
**Implementation:** NOT STARTED  
**Core impact:** None at this stage; do not modify the production core until the idea is researched and approved.

### Concept

Add a small, self-contained **18+ social / dating / connection module** inside XKiss as an optional feature, rather than creating a separate website.

Initial concept:

- 18+ profiles and age-gated access.
- Discovery by profile/location and other permitted filters.
- In-platform messaging.
- Optional live/video interaction as a separate capability.
- Privacy-first contact model; do not expose personal phone numbers or external contact details by default.
- Future monetization may be considered for permitted digital interactions/services, subject to legal, safety, payment-provider, and platform-policy review.

### Required review before implementation

This idea must be re-examined from the beginning before any code is added:

1. Current market and competitor research.
2. Applicable laws and jurisdiction requirements.
3. 18+ / age-verification requirements.
4. Safety, moderation, reporting, blocking, and abuse-prevention design.
5. Privacy and personal-data handling.
6. Messaging and live-video architecture.
7. Payment-provider and monetization constraints.
8. XKiss production-readiness impact.
9. Full technical design and isolated module boundary.
10. Build → real test → regression → GREEN → LOCK.

### Important rule

This is a **recorded future development idea only**. It does not authorize implementation, monetization, deployment, or changes to existing GREEN/LOCKED XKiss sections.

When revisited, inspect the real XKiss repository/runtime state first and perform a fresh feasibility and compliance review before implementation.
